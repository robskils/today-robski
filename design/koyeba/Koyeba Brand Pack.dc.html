01The idea
Koyeba is Lingala for “to know”. Set things down, and you’ll know where they are — they come into view when you need them.
Koyeba is a calm, private home for a whole life: mail, calendar, tasks, notes, messages, money and the inner life, joined up through Spaces.
The look follows the idea. Things are folded away until needed. Nothing shouts. One gold accent marks only what needs you.
Nasepeli koyeba yo — pleased to know you.

04Type
For anything that speaks
Cormorant Garamond
Good evening, Robin.
Light 300 for headings, names, numbers and quotes. Italic for the one word that turns gold. Medium 500 only for the wordmark.
For anything that labels
Jost
Coming into view
Regular 400 for body and interface, Medium 500 for eyebrows and buttons. Uppercase labels always get wide tracking (0.2–0.3em).

05Voice
Calm
Short sentences that breathe. No exclamation marks, no hype, no urgency theatre.
Warm
A trusted friend with good taste. Plain words for real life: “the kids”, “the move”.
British
Colour, programme, organise. Curly quotes, real dashes. Sentence case for headings.
Felt, not said
“For a life well lived” is a footer whisper. Show the care; don’t announce it.
