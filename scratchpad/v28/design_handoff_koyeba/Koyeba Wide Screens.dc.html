1Using the whole screen – one Today, three widths
The page shouldn’t be a fixed column pinned to the left. It should be a grid that gains columns as the screen widens: one on a phone, two on a laptop, three on a big screen, then it stops growing and centres – so lines never get too long to read.
Sections keep folding, and they move between columns rather than stretching. The sub-header with the boxed back arrow and “+ New” is gone (the breadcrumb and Set down cover both). No money on Today. Folding sections stay folded across widths.
