The idea

We carry too much at once.

Tasks half-remembered, ideas we meant to keep, money we meant to watch. Held loosely, it leaks – onto scraps of paper, into ten different apps, or just quietly lost.

Koyeba is Lingala for to know. Put something down once and you’ll always know where it is – the task when the day calls for it, the page when you’re planning, the thought when you pause.
