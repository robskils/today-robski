The idea

Your life, in Spaces

November 2026 · Feito em Lisboa · 4 min read

Your life is not one undifferentiated stream; it has parts. Work. Home. A side project. A person you look after. In most apps those parts are scattered across a dozen tools, and you are the one holding the connections in your head. In Kaya they have somewhere to live. A Space is a room for one part of your life, and everything that belongs there - the mail, the events, the tasks, the notes, the money - gathers inside it.

A room, not a label

A folder is a place you put things. A Space is more like a room you walk into: open it and you see that part of your life whole - what is coming up, what is waiting, what you wrote down, what it is costing. You are not filing; you are stepping into a context, with everything already there.

Everything joins up

Because a Space gathers across tools, the connections you used to keep in your head are made for you:

  • An email from a client sits beside the task it created and the event it led to.
  • A note about a trip sits with the trip's dates and its running cost.
  • A person's Space holds your history with them - messages, the last time you spoke, the thing you meant to follow up.

Nothing is copied or moved; each thing stays itself and simply shows up in the room it belongs to. And one item can belong to more than one Space when life is not tidy - a dinner that is both Family and a Birthday.

As private, or as shared, as you like

A Space is yours by default. Some are worth opening to other people - a household, a project, a trip - so you can share a wall, a list, the parts that are genuinely joint, and keep the rest to yourself. Sharing is per Space and per item, so letting someone into one room never opens the others.

Start with one

You do not have to map your whole life before you begin. Make a Space for the part that feels most scattered right now - the one you are forever chasing across apps - and let the mail, tasks and notes start landing in it. When it helps, make another. The structure grows to fit the life, not the other way round.

Most tools organise by the kind of thing - all your mail here, all your tasks there. Kaya can organise by the part of your life a thing belongs to. That is the difference between a filing cabinet and a home.

A Space asks nothing of you but a name. Give one to the part of your life that never sits still, and watch it come into view.
