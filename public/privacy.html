Daybook

Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

Daybook (daybook.fyi) is a private, calm home for your day - your calendar, tasks, notes, mail and more, in one place. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the control you have. Daybook is operated by Robin Lumley-Savile. Questions? contact@daybook.fyi.

What we collect

  • Your account - the email address you sign in with and the name you choose.
  • What you create - the tasks, notes, tables, events, journal entries and other content you add to Daybook, so we can store it and show it back to you.
  • Connected accounts - if you choose to connect a service (such as Google Calendar or an email mailbox), the credentials needed to access it on your behalf, stored encrypted.

Google user data

If you connect your Google Calendar, Daybook requests read and write access to your calendar events (the calendar.events scope) and your Google account email address, only so that:

  • your own calendar events can be shown inside Daybook alongside your other items, and
  • we can label the connection with the Google account you connected.

Limited Use. Daybook's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google Calendar data is used only to provide and improve the calendar features you see in Daybook. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties or used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

How your data is used

We use your data solely to run Daybook for you - to store your content, sign you in, show your calendar and mail, and deliver features you turn on (such as the morning brief). We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and analytics

The public pages of this site (home, privacy and terms) can use two analytics services - Google Analytics and VisitorTracking - to understand how the site is used so we can improve it. These set cookies and collect usage data such as pages viewed and approximate location. They load only if you accept in the cookie banner shown on your first visit; choose Decline and neither one runs. Your choice is remembered on your device, and you can change it any time by clearing this site's cookies. These analytics run on the marketing pages only - never inside your signed-in Daybook - and they never see your Daybook content.

Storage and security

Your data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Sensitive credentials - such as connected-account tokens and mailbox passwords - are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). Access is scoped to your account.

Sharing your data

We don't share your personal data with third parties, except the infrastructure and service providers strictly needed to run Daybook (for example Cloudflare for hosting, and Google's own APIs when you connect Google Calendar). Content you explicitly choose to share with another Daybook member is shared only as you direct.

Your control

  • Disconnect any service at any time (for Google Calendar, use the Disconnect button in the calendar view) - we revoke the token and delete it.
  • Export your data from Settings.
  • Delete your account from Settings, which removes your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
