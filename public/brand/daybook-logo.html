Using it
  • ✦One word. It is always “Lifebook”, never “Day book”. “book” leans into italic, tucked close to the roman “Day”.
  • ✦The mark is a sunrise over two page-lines - a book of days, a new day. It may stand alone where the name is already clear.
  • ✦Gold on paper, brighter on black. On light grounds the mark and word are the warm gold (#a8844a). On black - the favicon and app icon - the gold is lifted so it reads at a glance.
  • ✦Give it room. Keep clear space around the lockup of at least the height of the mark; never crowd it or set it on a busy background.
