Using it
  • ✦One word. It is always “Shizu”, never “Life book”. “book” leans into italic, set close to the roman “Life”.
  • ✦The mark is the monogram - a roman L, an italic b and a full stop, ringed. It is the wordmark in miniature, and may stand alone where the name is already clear.
  • ✦Set in Cormorant Garamond. The letterforms are drawn from the brand serif, so the mark and the wordmark are of one voice.
  • ✦Ink or gold on paper, brighter on black. On light grounds the mark is ink (#1b1820) or the warm gold (#a8844a). On black - the favicon and app icon - the gold is lifted so it reads at a glance.
  • ✦Give it room. Keep clear space around the lockup of at least the height of the mark; never crowd it or set it on a busy background.
