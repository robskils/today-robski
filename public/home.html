Tools

One set of tools, talking to each other.

For your inner world

A page for each day. A look back at the week, and a steer for the next. The few goals you're working towards. The books you mean to get to, and the bigger plans for one day.

For your outer world

An inbox you can clear in one pass. A diary that knows who's coming. What needs doing today, and what can wait. What the dog really costs.

A taste of what's in there. The rest is for you to find.

Spaces

Each part of your life gets a Space.

The kids, the house, the dog, the business. An email becomes a task, a payment files itself, an event finds its people - and all of it gathers in the Space it belongs to.

Open one part of your life to the people who live it with you - and only that part.
