The idea

A daybook is somewhere you set things down - so you can stop holding them in your head, and still trust them to come back.

  • Your daySchedule, tasks and appointments
  • Your habitsThe routines you're keeping up
  • Your reflectionsThoughts, feelings, lessons
  • Your to-dosUrgent now, and the long game

We carry too much at once. Tasks half-remembered, ideas we meant to keep, money we meant to watch, feelings we never sat with. So it leaks - onto sticky notes, into ten different apps, or just quietly lost.

Daybook is one place to put all of it. You capture quickly and let go, and Daybook brings the right piece back at the right moment - the task when the day needs it, the note when you're planning, the reflection when you pause.

Offload with confidence. Resurface with trust. That's the whole idea.

The Daybook ethos

The right thing, surfaced in the right place, at the right time.

Everything in Daybook bends towards that one idea - nothing shouts for attention, and nothing worth remembering slips away.

Nine tools, one place

One place for a whole life

Nine tools in one calm place. They work together, so nothing lives in a silo and nothing falls between them - all of it in service of a conscious and beautiful life.

☀

Today

A calm read on your day - what's on, what you're carrying, what matters now. The still point you start from.

✓

Tasks

Your day as calm streams, not a nagging list. Work with the flow when you're in it; nothing turns red.

◑

Calendar

Month, week and agenda in one view. It reads and writes your Google calendar, and events can count as practice.

▤

Notes

Write freely - a scribbled idea, a longer thought, anything worth keeping. Link it up and nothing gets lost.

Tables

Turn notes into structure when you're ready - a proper table for whatever is worth tracking, right where you wrote it.

◈

Life Areas

The heart of Daybook. Your streams, goals and spending all organise around what matters most.

✎

Reflection

A home for the inner stuff - journalling, insights, dreams, and gentle coaching when you want it.

💰

Money

See where the money goes, sorted by the areas of life you care about. Clear, quiet, no spreadsheets.

Mail

All your inboxes in one calm room - read, reply and search across every account, without breaking focus.

And the best part - the tools talk to each other.

Private by design

Yours. Genuinely.

Daybook holds the most personal parts of your life, so it treats them that way. Your data is private to you - not mined, not sold, not fed to anyone's model.

Want AI to help? You bring your own AI account. The connection runs straight between you and your provider - your call, on your terms.

  • ✓
    Your Daybook is yours - export it, keep it, own it.
  • ✓
    No adverts and no tracking. You're the person here, not the product.
  • ✓
    AI is opt-in and bring-your-own-key - a secure link between you and your AI provider.
  • ✓
    One private space per person, on your own corner of Daybook.
