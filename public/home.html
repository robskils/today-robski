Where it starts

A life is many parts at once - the doing and the dreaming, the urgent and the quiet. Koyeba gathers them into one calm place.

  • Your daySchedule, tasks and appointments
  • Your peopleThe ones you want to keep close
  • Your reflectionsThoughts, feelings, what it means
  • Your to-dosUrgent now, and the long game

We carry too much at once. Tasks half-remembered, ideas we meant to keep, money we meant to watch, feelings we never sat with. Held loosely, it leaks - onto scraps of paper, into ten different apps, or just quietly lost.

Koyeba is where it all comes in. You set something down and let go of it, and it comes back when you need it - the task when the day calls for it, the page when you're planning, the thought when you pause.

Set it down with confidence. Find it again with trust.

Your time, your people, your plans, your rhythms - and the scattered digital life wrapped around them. Gathered into one quiet place, and wholly yours.

Your whole suite

Every tool, in one place

Mail, calendar, pages, tasks, money and people for the doing; reflection, goals and Spaces for the inner life. Joined up so nothing slips, and handled quietly, so you stay on top of it.

Where it all meets

Spaces join the dots

Each part of your life gets a Space - a project, the family, your health, the business. Tag anything to one, and the mail, events, tasks, pages and spending for that part of life all gather there.

An email becomes a task. A page becomes a table. A payment files itself against the right Space. Nothing piles up in silos; it flows to where it belongs - so you hold your life lightly, and step around the overwhelm.

For the inner life

The inner life, included

Most tools take care of the doing and leave the rest of you outside. Koyeba keeps both close - the outer life of tasks and plans, the inner life of thoughts, feelings and what it all means - so each makes more sense of the other. Journalling, reviews, gentle coaching and quiet reflection tools aren't bolted-on extras; they're how you bring a little awareness to the ordinary days, and notice the life you're actually living. There when you need them, out of the way when you don't.

Private by design

Yours. Securely.

Koyeba holds the most personal parts of your life - your reflections, your relationships. So it's locked down properly: two-factor sign-in, your data encrypted at rest.

And your data? We don't want it. We're building a business on trust, not on selling you - so nothing of yours is mined, sold, or fed to anyone's model. It's yours. Export the lot in one tap, keep it on your own storage if you'd rather, bring your own AI - the choice is always yours.
