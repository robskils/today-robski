Need a hand? Email contact@lifefold.co and a real person will get back to you. Tell us what you were doing and what you expected to happen, and a screenshot if you have one.

Getting started

  • Open LifeFold and sign in with your email - we send a six-digit code, no password to remember.
  • Today is your day at a glance. Home gathers everything else.
  • Add a task, note or event from the buttons on Home, or from each section.
  • Group your life into a few life areas, each in its own colour.

Your calendar

  • Month, week and agenda views. Tap a day's + to add an event to it.
  • Events can repeat. Open a repeating event to change or remove the repeat.
  • Connect your Google Calendar, or subscribe to your country's public holidays and your team's fixtures in Settings › Calendar.

Your privacy

Your LifeFold is private to you. We never sell your data or use it to train a model, and you can export everything at any time. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Account & billing

  • LifeFold has a free tier. Paid plans and your account live in Settings › Account and Settings › Plan.
  • To close your account and remove your data, use Close account in Settings, or email us.
